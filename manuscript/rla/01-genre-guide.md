# Genre Guide

STAAR passages come from many genres, and each has its own features to notice. Knowing the genre tells
you what to look for. *(Standards 8A–8E.)*

---

## Literary Genres

**Fiction ○ (8A)** — made-up stories: realistic fiction, historical fiction, mystery, adventure,
humor, fantasy, science fiction, short stories.
- *Notice:* characters, setting, conflict, plot, and theme.

**Literary Nonfiction** — true stories told with literary craft (memoir, personal essay).
- *Notice:* the author's real experience and the message drawn from it.

**Poetry ○ (8B)** — written in lines and stanzas; forms include epic, lyric, and humorous poetry.
- *Notice:* **graphical elements** such as line length and punctuation, plus rhythm, rhyme, and imagery.

**Drama ○ (8C)** — plays meant to be performed, organized in **acts and scenes** with dialogue and
stage directions.
- *Notice:* how acts and scenes move the action forward.

---

## Informational and Argumentative Genres

**Informational ○/★ (8D)** — explains a topic.
- *Notice:* the **controlling idea or thesis** with supporting evidence ★; text features such as
  **footnotes, endnotes, and citations** ○; and the **organizational pattern** (cause–effect,
  compare–contrast, sequence, problem–solution) ○.

**Argumentative ★ (8E)** — makes a claim and defends it.
- *Notice:* the **claim** and the argument ★; the **counter-argument** ★; and the **intended
  audience** ○.

**Persuasive** — tries to convince the reader to think or act a certain way.
- *Notice:* appeals, word choice, and calls to action.

---

## For Revising & Editing

**Correspondence** — letters and emails, friendly or business.
- *Notice:* greeting, purpose, tone, and closing. (You revise and edit these in the Writing section.)

---

> **Strategy:** The first thing to ask about any passage is *"What genre is this, and what is its
> purpose?"* A story wants you to follow characters and theme; an argument wants you to weigh a claim
> and its evidence. Reading with the genre in mind makes every question easier.
