# Part 2 · Reading Language Arts
## How Grade 8 RLA Is Built

The Grade 8 Reading Language Arts test has about **45 questions worth 56 points** and comes in two
sections, plus written responses. This book teaches **every reading and writing skill the test
measures.**

| Section | What It Covers | On the Test |
|---|---|---|
| **Reading** | Vocabulary, comprehension, literary elements, genres, author's craft; includes **2 Short Constructed Responses** | ~28 questions / ~30 points |
| **Writing — Revising & Editing** | Organizing and improving writing; grammar and conventions; includes **1 Extended Constructed Response** | ~17 questions / ~26 points |

### Three Kinds of Tasks
1. **Multiple-choice and tech-enhanced questions** — about passages you read.
2. **Short Constructed Responses (SCR)** — a few written sentences with text evidence (2 points each).
3. **Extended Constructed Response (ECR)** — a full essay (10 points), tied to a reading passage.

### Genres You Will Read
- **Literary:** fiction, literary nonfiction, poetry, drama
- **Informational / argumentative:** informational, argumentative, persuasive
- **In revising & editing you will also see:** correspondence (letters, emails)

### Readiness vs. Supporting
As in math, each skill is **★ Readiness** (tested heavily — master first) or **○ Supporting** (tested
less often). The skill tags appear throughout the lessons.

> **Key Idea:** Reading STAAR is not about memorizing stories — it is about **using evidence.** Almost
> every question can be answered by pointing to a specific place in the text. Learn to read closely and
> back up every answer with proof.
