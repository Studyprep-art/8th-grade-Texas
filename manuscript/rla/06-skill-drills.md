# Reading & Writing Warm-Up Drills

Quick, focused drills to sharpen one skill at a time before a full-length test. Each item names the
TEKS it practices. Answers are at the end.

## Vocabulary  (2B, 2C)
1. In "The storm was *relentless*, never stopping for days," *relentless* means — A. gentle  B. unending  C. short  D. quiet
2. The root *path* (feeling) shows that *sympathy* means — A. a feeling with or for another  B. a path  C. a star  D. a leader
3. The root *duc* means *lead*, so *conduct* most likely means — A. to follow  B. to lead or guide  C. to stop  D. to break

## Comprehension & Inference  (5F, 5G)
4. A character "grabbed an umbrella and sighed at the gray sky." You can infer that — A. it is sunny  B. it may rain  C. she is joyful  D. it is midnight
5. The sentence that states what a paragraph is mostly about is the — A. minor detail  B. central idea  C. quotation  D. title

## Author's Craft  (9A, 9D, 9G)
6. "The wind *whispered* through the trees" is an example of — A. simile  B. personification  C. hyperbole  D. irony
7. "As busy as a bee" is a — A. metaphor  B. simile  C. fact  D. fallacy
8. An author who writes to convince you to recycle has the purpose to — A. entertain  B. inform  C. persuade  D. confuse
9. "Everybody owns this phone, so you should too" is which fallacy? — A. bandwagon  B. circular reasoning  C. analogy  D. irony

## Literary Elements  (7B, 7C)
10. A scene that jumps back to a character's childhood is a — A. flashback  B. climax  C. setting  D. theme
11. An early hint about a later event is — A. irony  B. foreshadowing  C. mood  D. plot

## Revising  (10B, 10C)
12. Which transition shows contrast? — A. In addition  B. However  C. For example  D. First
13. The best sentence to add a detail is one that — A. repeats the topic  B. gives a specific fact or example  C. changes the subject  D. asks a question

## Editing  (10D)
14. "The team of players *are* ready." The best change is — A. *are* → *is*  B. *team* → *teams*  C. *players* → *player*  D. no change
15. "*Its* going to rain." The best change is — A. *Its* → *It's*  B. *going* → *gone*  C. *rain* → *Rain*  D. no change
16. "*Me and him* went." The best change is — A. *Me and him* → *He and I*  B. *went* → *gone*  C. no change  D. add a comma
17. "*There* going to *their* house." The first word should be — A. *They're*  B. *Their*  C. no change  D. deleted
18. "We *seen* the movie." The best change is — A. *seen* → *saw*  B. *movie* → *movies*  C. no change  D. *We* → *Us*
