# Part 4 · Diagnose & Grow

Scoring a test is only step one. The real gains come from seeing **which skills need work** and
studying those next. Use these pages after every practice test.

---

### Rough Level Guide *(estimate only)*
| If you earned about… | Estimated level |
|---|---|
| Less than ~35% | Did Not Meet Grade Level |
| ~35%–55% | Approaches Grade Level |
| ~55%–80% | Meets Grade Level |
| ~80% or more | Masters Grade Level |

> **Important:** The State of Texas sets the official passing scores each year, and they change. These
> percentages are a **rough guide** to track your progress, not an official score. Aim for **Meets** or
> higher.

## Strand Diagnostic Chart

For each test, count how many you got right **in each strand or section**. A low percentage points you
straight to the lesson to review.

### Mathematics — count correct per strand
| Strand | Questions on each test | If this is low, review… |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Numerical Representations & Relationships | ~4 | Strand 1 lesson |
| 2 · Computations & Algebraic Relationships | ~15 | Strand 2 lesson |
| 3 · Geometry & Measurement | ~13 | Strand 3 lesson |
| 4 · Data Analysis & Personal Financial Literacy | ~8 | Strand 4 lesson |

### Reading Language Arts — count correct per skill area
| Skill area | If this is low, review… |
|---|---|
| Vocabulary (context clues, roots) | Reading Lessons, Part A |
| Comprehension & inference | Reading Lessons, Part B |
| Using text evidence / summarizing | Reading Lessons, Part C |
| Literary elements (theme, character, plot) | Reading Lessons, Part D |
| Author's purpose & craft | Reading Lessons, Part E |
| Revising & Editing | Revising & Editing Refresher |
| Short / Extended responses | SCR & ECR Mastery |

> **How to use it:** After each test, find your **weakest** row, reread that lesson, redo its "Try It"
> items, then try the next practice test. This is the fastest way to raise your score.
