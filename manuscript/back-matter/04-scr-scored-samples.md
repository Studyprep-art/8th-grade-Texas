# Short Constructed Response — Scored Samples

Below are real-style student responses scored on the **2-point** rubric, with notes on what earns and
loses points. Reminder — a 2-point answer = **Answer + Evidence + Explanation.**

---

## Prompt (from RLA Test 1, SCR 1)
*How does Maya change from the beginning to the end of the passage? Support your answer with evidence
from the text.*

**Score 2 — Full credit**
> At the start, Maya is afraid and almost quits—she "almost reached for her bag, ready to leave." By
> the end, she has faced her fear and feels proud: "She was smiling," and she "played anyway." Maya
> changes from fearful to confident.

*Why it earns 2:* a clear answer (fearful → confident), **two pieces of text evidence**, and an
explanation that connects them.

**Score 1 — Partial credit**
> Maya was scared, but then she played the piano and felt better at the end.

*Why it earns 1:* the idea is correct, but there is **no text evidence** and only a thin explanation.

**Score 0 — No credit**
> I think Maya is a good piano player, and I like music too.

*Why it earns 0:* it does **not answer the question** about how she changes and uses no evidence.

## Prompt (from RLA Test 4, SCR 2)
*Explain one way fungi help a forest. Use evidence from the passage.*

**Score 2 — Full credit**
> Fungi help a forest by feeding trees. The text says the threads "connect to the roots of trees,
> trading nutrients for sugar." This means fungi give trees nutrients they need to grow.

*Why it earns 2:* clear answer, an exact quotation as evidence, and an explanation.

**Score 1 — Partial credit**
> Fungi help trees grow in the forest.

*Why it earns 1:* a correct point, but **no evidence** and no explanation.

**Score 0 — No credit**
> Mushrooms can be food for people.

*Why it earns 0:* off-topic — it does not explain how fungi help a **forest**.

---

> **Self-check for every SCR:** Did I (1) answer the exact question, (2) include a specific detail or
> quotation from the text, and (3) explain how it supports my answer? If all three are yes, you have a
> 2-point response.
