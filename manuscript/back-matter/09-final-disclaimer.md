# Final Notes

## You Are Ready

If you have worked through the lessons, taken the practice tests, reviewed the explanations, and used
the diagnostic charts, you have done the hard, honest work that builds real confidence. You have
practiced every skill the STAAR Grade 8 Reading Language Arts and Mathematics tests can ask of
you—reading closely, reasoning with evidence, and solving real problems.

On test day, trust your preparation. Read carefully, answer every question, give the written responses
your best effort, and use your full time. You've got this.

## A Word About What Comes Next

Spring 2027 is the final STAAR administration for grades 3–8. A new Texas assessment will follow, but
the skills you have strengthened here do not expire. Strong readers and problem-solvers succeed on any
test—and far beyond it.
