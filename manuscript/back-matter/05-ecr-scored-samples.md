# Extended Constructed Response — Scored Samples

Below is one ECR prompt answered at three score levels, with notes. The **10-point** rubric rewards a
clear claim/central idea, coherent organization, specific evidence that is explained, and correct
language and conventions.

---

## Prompt (from RLA Test 1, ECR)
*Passage 2, "Cities Buzz Again," describes the benefits of urban beekeeping. Should your community
start an urban beekeeping or rooftop-garden program? Write a multi-paragraph argumentative essay...*

---

### Score 8–10 — High

> Cities should start urban beekeeping and rooftop-garden programs because they make communities
> healthier, greener, and more connected.
>
> First, bees and gardens improve the environment. According to "Cities Buzz Again," a single hive
> "can pollinate millions of flowers in a day," leading to "healthier parks, gardens, and street
> trees." Greener neighborhoods mean cleaner air for everyone.
>
> Second, these programs bring people together. The passage explains that "neighbors who tend a shared
> hive learn about nature and work together," and local honey "supports small businesses." A community
> that grows together grows stronger.
>
> Some people worry that beekeeping is dangerous or difficult. It is true that "beekeeping takes
> training." However, with proper instruction, hives are safe, and schools already use them to teach
> science. The benefits clearly outweigh the risks.
>
> For these reasons, my community should start an urban beekeeping or rooftop-garden program. It would
> make our city healthier, greener, and more united.

*Why it scores high:* a **clear claim**, two organized body paragraphs each with **evidence from the
passage** that is explained, a **counter-argument** that is answered, a strong conclusion, and correct
conventions.

---

### Score 4–7 — Middle

> I think cities should have bees because they help plants. The article says bees pollinate flowers,
> and that is good for gardens. Also, honey is nice to have. Some people don't like bees, but they are
> okay. Cities should try beekeeping.

*Why it scores in the middle:* it states a **position** and includes **one** piece of evidence, but the
ideas are **thinly developed**, the organization is weak (one paragraph), and the counter-argument is
vague.

---

### Score 1–3 — Low

> Bees are good. I like gardens. Cities should do it because honey is sweet.

*Why it scores low:* there is a position but **no real evidence** from the passage, **no organization**,
and almost no development.

---

> **Planning pays off.** Before writing, jot your claim and two reasons with the evidence for each. An
> organized essay with explained evidence almost always scores higher than a longer, unplanned one.
> Leave a minute at the end to check spelling and punctuation.
