# Grammar & Conventions Quick Reference

Everything the STAAR Grade 8 editing questions check, on one page. *(Standard 10D.)*

## Sentences  (10D·i)
- **Complete sentence:** has a subject and a verb and expresses a complete thought.
- **Fragment (wrong):** "Running to the bus." → Add a subject/verb: "She was running to the bus."
- **Run-on / comma splice (wrong):** "I was tired, I slept." → Fix with a period, semicolon, or conjunction: "I was tired, so I slept."

## Subject-Verb Agreement  (10D·i)
- The verb matches the **subject**, not a word in between.
- "The **box** of markers **is** here." (subject = *box*, singular) — ignore *of markers*.
- Singular subjects → singular verbs (*is, has, runs*); plural → plural (*are, have, run*).

## Verb Tense & Voice  (10D·ii)
- Keep tense **consistent**: don't switch from past to present without reason.
- Use correct forms: *saw* (not "seen"), *gone* (not "went" after *have*).

## Pronouns  (10D·iii, iv)
- **Case:** use *I, he, she, we, they* as subjects; *me, him, her, us, them* as objects. "He and **I** went." / "between you and **me**."
- **Agreement:** a pronoun matches its noun in number: "Each student brought **his or her** book."

## Capitalization  (10D·v)
- Capitalize the first word of a sentence, proper nouns, and *I*.

## Punctuation  (10D·vi)
- **Comma** around a nonrestrictive phrase: "My dog, a beagle, barks."
- **Semicolon** joins two related sentences: "It rained; we stayed in."
- **Colon** introduces a list: "Bring three things: a pen, paper, and a book."
- **Parentheses** add extra information: "The test (held in April) was long."

## Commonly Confused Words  (10D·vii)
| Word | Means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| its / it's | belonging to it / it is | *The dog wagged its tail. It's raining.* |
| there / their / they're | a place / belonging to them / they are | *There it is. Their house. They're here.* |
| to / two / too | toward / 2 / also | *Go to class. Two pens. Me too.* |
| affect / effect | to change (verb) / a result (noun) | *Rain affects plants. The effect was clear.* |
| your / you're | belonging to you / you are | *Your book. You're right.* |
| our / are | belonging to us / verb | *Our class. We are ready.* |
| whole / hole | entire / an opening | *The whole day. A hole in the sock.* |
| a lot | *(two words)* many | *A lot of people came.* |
