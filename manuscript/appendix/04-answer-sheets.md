# Practice Answer Sheets

The real STAAR test is taken online, but marking your answers on these sheets while you practice makes
scoring quick and keeps your work organized. For each multiple-choice question, fill in the letter of
your answer. For **griddable, equation-editor, and "Select TWO" items**, write your answer in the box
or circle both letters. Write your **SCR and ECR responses on separate lined paper.**

After the test, score with the **Quick Answer Keys**, then fill in your **Score-Tracking Sheet** and
**Strand Diagnostic Chart**.

*(Reproducible answer grids for Math Tests 1–4 and RLA Tests 1–4 follow. Each grid has rows for every
question, with bubbles A–D and boxes for written-in answers.)*
