## Passage 1 — The Last Lap (fiction)

(1) Diego's legs burned as he rounded the final turn. Three runners were ahead of him, and the finish
line seemed impossibly far.

(2) He thought about quitting. His lungs ached; his side cramped. No one would blame him for slowing
down—he was only in eighth grade, and this was the district final.

(3) Then he heard it: his little sister's voice in the crowd, shouting his name. She had watched him
train every morning before dawn, timing his laps with their mother's old stopwatch.

(4) Something shifted. Diego lengthened his stride. One runner fell behind, then another. His legs
found a rhythm he did not know he had.

(5) He did not win—he crossed the line in second place. But as he bent over, gasping, his sister threw
her arms around him. "You passed two people!" she cried.

(6) Diego smiled through the pain. He had not come in first, but he had given everything he had. Some
races, he realized, you win just by not giving up.

**1.** "The finish line seemed *impossibly far*" shows that Diego felt it was — A. easy to reach  B. very hard to reach  C. right in front of him  D. behind him
**2.** What makes Diego push harder? — A. the coach  B. his sister's voice  C. the weather  D. a trophy
**3.** "Something shifted" suggests that Diego — A. gave up  B. found new motivation  C. stopped running  D. was injured
**4.** A theme of the story is that — A. winning is all that matters  B. effort and perseverance matter  C. running is easy  D. crowds are loud
**5.** At the end, Diego's feelings are best described as — A. bitter  B. proud despite not winning  C. angry  D. bored
**6.** Which line **best** shows Diego's determination? — A. "He thought about quitting"  B. "Diego lengthened his stride"  C. "the finish line seemed impossibly far"  D. "his lungs ached"
**7.** "His legs found a rhythm" is an example of — A. simile  B. personification  C. hyperbole  D. irony
**SCR 1.** *(2 points)* How does Diego change during the race? Support your answer with **evidence from the text.**

## Passage 2 — The Clever Octopus (informational)

(1) The octopus may be the smartest animal without a backbone. These sea creatures can solve puzzles,
use tools, and even recognize individual people.

(2) An octopus has about 500 million neurons—more than a rabbit. Strangely, two-thirds of them are not
in its brain but in its eight arms. Each arm can taste, touch, and make decisions almost on its own.

(3) Octopuses are famous escape artists. In aquariums, they have opened jars, squeezed through tiny
gaps, and sneaked out of tanks at night to hunt. One octopus learned to unscrew a jar lid to reach a
crab inside.

(4) They also protect themselves cleverly. An octopus can change color and texture in an instant to
blend into rocks or coral, and it can squirt ink to confuse a predator.

(5) Scientists are still learning how such a different kind of mind works. The octopus reminds us that
intelligence can take many surprising forms.

**8.** The central idea of the passage is that octopuses are — A. dangerous  B. surprisingly intelligent  C. always in jars  D. animals with backbones
**9.** As used in paragraph 3, "escape artists" suggests octopuses are good at — A. painting  B. getting out of tight spaces  C. swimming fast  D. making ink
**10.** Paragraph 2 is mainly organized by — A. telling a story  B. giving facts about the octopus's nervous system  C. comparing colors  D. listing dangers
**11.** Which detail **best** supports the idea that octopuses use tools? — A. "change color"  B. "learned to unscrew a jar lid"  C. "squirt ink"  D. "500 million neurons"
**12.** From paragraph 2, you can infer that an octopus's arms — A. are useless  B. can act with some independence  C. cannot move  D. are its only brain
**13.** The author's purpose is to — A. persuade you to buy an octopus  B. inform readers about octopus intelligence  C. tell a scary story  D. sell aquariums
**14.** Which is the **best** summary? — A. Octopuses live in the sea.  B. Octopuses are highly intelligent invertebrates that solve problems, use tools, and defend themselves cleverly.  C. Octopuses have ink.  D. Rabbits have neurons.
**SCR 2.** *(2 points)* Give **one** example that shows octopuses are intelligent. Use **evidence from the passage.**
