# Warm-Up Skill Drills

Short, mixed drills to warm up before the full-length tests. Do a set, then check the answer key at
the end. Each item names the TEKS it practices.

---

## Strand 1 — Numerical Representations and Relationships
1. **(8.2D)** Order from least to greatest: 0.45, ⅖, √0.2, 43%.
2. **(8.2B)** Between which two whole numbers is √72?
3. **(8.2C)** Write 0.00056 in scientific notation.
4. **(8.2A)** Is √81 rational or irrational?
5. **(8.2C)** Write 3.2 × 10⁵ in standard form.

## Strand 2 — Computations and Algebraic Relationships
6. **(8.4C)** Find the slope of the line through (2, 3) and (6, 11).
7. **(8.5I)** A tank holds 50 L and drains 4 L per minute. Write an equation for the amount y after x minutes.
8. **(8.5G)** Is {(2, 4), (3, 9), (2, 5)} a function?
9. **(8.8C)** Solve 5x − 2 = 3x + 8.
10. **(8.5F)** Is y = 6x proportional or non-proportional?

## Strand 3 — Geometry and Measurement
11. **(8.7C)** A right triangle has legs 9 and 12. Find the hypotenuse.
12. **(8.7A)** Find the volume of a cylinder with radius 5 and height 4. (Use π ≈ 3.14.)
13. **(8.10C)** Reflect the point (−3, 7) over the y-axis.
14. **(8.3C)** Dilate (4, −6) by scale factor ½ about the origin.
15. **(8.8D)** Two angles of a triangle are 42° and 73°. Find the third angle.

## Strand 4 — Data Analysis and Personal Financial Literacy
16. **(8.12D)** Find the simple interest on $500 at 4% for 3 years.
17. **(8.11B)** Find the mean absolute deviation of 2, 4, 6, 8.
18. **(8.5D)** A trend line is y = 2x + 3. Predict y when x = 10.
19. **(8.11A)** Points rise from lower-left to upper-right. Name the association.
20. **(8.12A)** True or false: a longer loan at the same rate usually costs more in total interest.
