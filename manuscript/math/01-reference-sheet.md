# Grade 8 Mathematics Reference Sheet

On the real STAAR test, these formulas are provided for you. Learn where each one is so you can find
it fast. (This replicates the key formulas from the STAAR Grade 8 Mathematics Reference Materials.)

### Perimeter & Circumference
- Circle: **C = 2πr** or **C = πd**

### Area
- Triangle: **A = ½bh**
- Rectangle / Parallelogram: **A = bh**
- Trapezoid: **A = ½(b₁ + b₂)h**
- Circle: **A = πr²**

### Surface Area  (B = area of the base, P = perimeter of the base)
- Prism — Lateral: **S = Ph** · Total: **S = Ph + 2B**
- Cylinder — Lateral: **S = 2πrh** · Total: **S = 2πrh + 2πr²**

### Volume  (B = area of the base)
- Prism / Cylinder: **V = Bh**
- Cone: **V = ⅓Bh**
- Sphere: **V = 4⁄3 πr³**

### Algebra & Graphing
- Slope of a line: **m = (y₂ − y₁) / (x₂ − x₁)**
- Slope-intercept form: **y = mx + b**
- Direct variation: **y = kx**
- Pythagorean theorem: **a² + b² = c²**

### Personal Financial Literacy
- Simple interest: **I = Prt**
- Compound interest: **A = P(1 + r⁄n)^(nt)**

### Useful Constants & Conversions
- **π ≈ 3.14** (or 22⁄7)
- Length (metric): 1 m = 100 cm = 1000 mm · 1 km = 1000 m
- Length (customary): 1 ft = 12 in · 1 yd = 3 ft · 1 mi = 5,280 ft

> **Strategy:** You do **not** need to memorize these — but you must know they exist and how to use
> them. In every worked example that uses a formula, this book shows you which one and why.
