# Mathematics · Practice Test 2

**40 questions · Calculator allowed · Use the Grade 8 Mathematics Reference Sheet**
Read each question carefully. Watch for directions like *Select TWO* and *least to greatest*.

---

## Reporting Category 1 · Numerical Representations and Relationships

**1.** Which list is ordered from **least to greatest**?
A. 0.72, ¾, √0.6, 78%  B. ¾, 0.72, 78%, √0.6  C. 78%, √0.6, ¾, 0.72  D. √0.6, 0.72, ¾, 78%

**2.** Between which two whole numbers does √90 lie?
A. 8 and 9  B. 9 and 10  C. 44 and 46  D. 89 and 91

**3.** *(Griddable)* Write 0.00045 in scientific notation. ________

**4.** Which number is **rational**?
A. √8  B. √16  C. π  D. √2

## Reporting Category 2 · Computations and Algebraic Relationships

**5.** What is the slope of the line through (−2, 1) and (2, 9)?
A. 1  B. 2  C. 4  D. 8

**6.** A proportional relationship passes through the origin and the point (5, 15). What is the unit rate?
A. 3  B. 5  C. 10  D. 15

**7.** *(Equation editor)* A candle is 20 cm tall and burns down 2 cm each hour. Write an equation for its height *y* after *x* hours. ________

**8.** Which relation is a **function**?
A. {(2, 1), (2, 3), (4, 5)}  B. {(1, 6), (2, 6), (3, 7)}  C. {(5, 2), (5, 4), (6, 8)}  D. {(0, 0), (0, 1), (1, 2)}

**9.** Which equation represents a **non-proportional** relationship?
A. y = 3x  B. y = 7x  C. y = 3x + 4  D. y = ½x

**10.** *(Griddable)* For a direct variation, y = 36 when x = 4. What is *y* when x = 7? ________

**11.** Which table shows a **proportional** relationship?
A. (1, 2)(2, 5)(3, 8)  B. (1, 4)(2, 8)(3, 12)  C. (1, 3)(2, 5)(3, 7)  D. (2, 7)(3, 9)(4, 11)

**12.** *(Griddable)* Solve for x: 7x − 5 = 3x + 19. ________

**13.** "Five more than three times a number equals twice the number plus twelve." Which equation models this?
A. 3x + 5 = 2x + 12  B. 5x + 3 = 12x + 2  C. 3x − 5 = 2x + 12  D. 5 + 3 = 2x + 12

**14.** Two lines are graphed and intersect at (−3, 2). What is the solution of the system?
A. x = 2, y = −3  B. x = −3, y = 2  C. x = −1  D. No solution

**15.** On a line, right triangles are drawn to two different pairs of points. What must be true of the triangles?
A. They are congruent  B. They are **similar**, so the slope is the same  C. They have equal area  D. They are isosceles

**16.** Which situation represents a **proportional** relationship?
A. A plumber charges $50 plus $40 per hour  B. A runner travels 6 miles per hour at a steady pace
C. A tank has 30 L and loses 5 L per hour  D. A gym costs $25 to join plus $5 per class

**17.** Which situation can be modeled by 4x + 6 = 2x + 18?
A. Two accounts start at $6 and $18 and grow $4 and $2 per week; when are they equal?
B. The product of 4 and 6  C. A rectangle's perimeter  D. 4 plus 6 plus 18

**18.** A taxi charges $4 to start plus $3 per mile. Which equation gives the cost *y* for *x* miles?
A. y = 4x + 3  B. y = 3x + 4  C. y = 7x  D. y = 3x − 4

**19.** *(Select TWO)* Which statements about the line y = −3x + 6 are true?
A. The slope is −3  B. The slope is 6  C. The y-intercept is 6  D. The y-intercept is −3

## Reporting Category 3 · Geometry and Measurement

**20.** A cylinder has radius 5 and height 6. What is its volume? (π ≈ 3.14)
A. 150  B. 235.5  C. 471  D. 942

**21.** A sphere has radius 3. What is its volume? (π ≈ 3.14)
A. 28.26  B. 84.78  C. 113.04  D. 339.12

**22.** A cylinder has radius 2 and height 10. What is its **total** surface area? (π ≈ 3.14)
A. 125.6  B. 150.72  C. 175.84  D. 251.2

**23.** *(Griddable)* A right triangle has legs of 9 and 40. What is the length of the hypotenuse? ________

**24.** What is the distance between (0, 0) and (6, 8)?
A. 7  B. 10  C. 14  D. 48

**25.** The point (4, −3) is dilated by a scale factor of 2 about the origin. What is the image?
A. (6, −1)  B. (8, −6)  C. (2, −1.5)  D. (8, −3)

**26.** The point (−6, 2) is reflected over the x-axis. What is the image?
A. (6, 2)  B. (−6, −2)  C. (6, −2)  D. (2, −6)

**27.** The point (4, 1) is rotated 180° about the origin. What is the image?
A. (−4, −1)  B. (−1, 4)  C. (1, −4)  D. (4, −1)

**28.** Which transformation **preserves** congruence (keeps the same size)?
A. Dilation by factor 2  B. Dilation by factor ½  C. Translation  D. Dilation by factor 3

**29.** Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. One angle measures 65°. What is the measure of its corresponding angle?
A. 25°  B. 65°  C. 115°  D. 180°

**30.** Two similar triangles have corresponding sides in a ratio of 3 : 4. A side of the smaller triangle is 9. What is the matching side of the larger?
A. 10  B. 11  C. 12  D. 13

**31.** The Pythagorean theorem relates the sides of a right triangle by which equation?
A. a + b = c  B. a² + b² = c²  C. a² − b² = c²  D. 2a + 2b = c

**32.** A rectangle measures 5 by 3. It is dilated by a scale factor of 2. What is the area of the image?
A. 30  B. 45  C. 60  D. 90

## Reporting Category 4 · Data Analysis and Personal Financial Literacy

**33.** *(Griddable)* Find the mean absolute deviation of 4, 8, 10, 14. ________

**34.** A trend line is modeled by y = 3x + 5. Predict *y* when x = 6.
A. 18  B. 20  C. 23  D. 30

**35.** A scatterplot shows points with no clear pattern or direction. What association is shown?
A. Positive linear  B. Negative linear  C. No association  D. Strong linear

**36.** Which description suggests a **non-linear** association?
A. Points in a straight upward line  B. Points in a straight downward line
C. Points following a curved, U-shaped path  D. Points exactly on one line

**37.** *(Griddable)* Find the simple interest on $1,200 at 6% for 3 years. ________

**38.** For the same loan amount and length, how does a **higher** interest rate affect the total cost?
A. It lowers the cost  B. It raises the cost  C. It has no effect  D. It cancels the loan

**39.** Which plan is likely to grow the **most** over time?
A. Saving nothing  B. Saving a little, once  C. Saving small amounts regularly with compound interest  D. Spending it all

**40.** College costs $16,000. A family expects to contribute $6,000 and wants to save the rest over 20 months. How much must they save each month?
A. $400  B. $500  C. $600  D. $800
