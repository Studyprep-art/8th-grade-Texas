# Welcome to Your STAAR Grade 8 Study Guide

Dear Student,

The STAAR Grade 8 tests in Reading Language Arts and Mathematics can feel like a lot — two subjects,
many skills, a timed online screen, and even written essays. This book exists to make all of that
feel familiar long before test day.

Here is the promise of this book: **everything inside is on the test, and nothing on the test is left
out.** Every lesson, every practice question, and every writing prompt is tied directly to the Texas
Essential Knowledge and Skills (TEKS) that STAAR measures at Grade 8. You will not waste a single
minute on material that will not help you.

You will move through four stages:

1. **Learn** — short, clear lessons that teach every tested skill.
2. **Practice** — quick drills so each skill sticks.
3. **Simulate** — eight full-length practice tests that look and feel like the real thing.
4. **Diagnose** — answer explanations and charts that show you exactly what to review next.

Work at a steady pace, check every answer explanation (even for questions you got right), and use the
diagnostic charts to spend your study time where it matters most. Confidence on test day comes from
practice, and this book gives you plenty.

You can do this. Let's get started.

— Andrian Ross
