# Test-Day Game Plan

You have practiced the skills and the question types. Test day is simply one more practice test — this
time for real. Follow this plan to stay calm and finish strong.

## The Night Before
- Pack what you need and go to bed on time. Rest helps your brain more than last-minute cramming.
- Do a light review of your **diagnostic charts** only — no new material.

## The Morning Of
- Eat a good breakfast. Arrive early so you feel settled, not rushed.
- Take a few slow breaths before you begin. You are ready.

## During the Test — Your Pacing Plan
1. **Read the directions of each question carefully.** Watch for words like *select two*, *least to
   greatest*, and *best*.
2. **Answer the questions you know first.** Flag harder ones and return to them — do not let one
   question eat your time.
3. **Use your tools.** On Math, use the **calculator** and the **reference sheet**; show your work on
   scratch paper. On Reading, use the online **dictionary** when a word blocks your understanding.
4. **Eliminate wrong answers.** Crossing out two choices doubles your odds on a tricky question.
5. **Never leave a question blank.** There is no penalty for guessing. Make your best choice and flag
   it to revisit if time allows.

## Budgeting Time for Written Responses (RLA)
- The **SCR** answers should be short and direct — make your point, give **text evidence**, done.
- Save enough time for the **ECR** essay (worth 10 points). A quick plan — thesis, two or three
  supporting points with evidence, a conclusion — keeps you organized. A partial, organized essay
  still earns points; a blank one earns none.

## Before You Submit
- Return to every flagged question. Make sure each one has an answer.
- Check that multiselect questions have the **right number** of choices marked.
- For written responses, reread once for clear sentences, correct spelling, and punctuation.

---

> **Key Idea:** Finish every question with *some* answer, give the written responses real effort, and
> use your full time. Steady and complete beats fast and blank.

> **You've got this.** Now turn the page and start building your skills.
