# Your Study Calendars

Pick the plan that fits the time you have. Each plan uses the same four stages — **Learn, Practice,
Simulate, Diagnose** — and spreads the eight full-length tests so you are never cramming. Check off
each day as you go.

> **Key Idea:** Short, steady sessions beat one long rush. Aim for 30–45 focused minutes per study day.

---

## The 4-Week Sprint (test is coming soon)

| Week | Focus | Do This |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Math — Strands 1 & 2 | Read lessons; do warm-up drills; take **Math Test 1**; review every explanation |
| **2** | Math — Strands 3 & 4 | Read lessons; drills; take **Math Tests 2 & 3**; fill diagnostic charts; redo weak skills |
| **3** | RLA — Reading + SCR | Genre guide & reading lessons; **SCR Mastery**; take **RLA Tests 1 & 2** |
| **4** | RLA — Writing + ECR, then finish | Revising/editing refresher; **ECR Mastery**; take **Math Test 4**, **RLA Tests 3 & 4**; final review of weakest strands |

## The 8-Week Plan (more time, deeper mastery)

| Week | Focus | Do This |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Math Strand 1 — Numerical | Lesson + drills; begin a daily routine |
| **2** | Math Strand 2 — Computations & Algebraic | Lesson + drills; **Math Test 1** + review |
| **3** | Math Strand 3 — Geometry & Measurement | Lesson + drills; **Math Test 2** + diagnostic chart |
| **4** | Math Strand 4 — Data & Financial Literacy | Lesson + drills; **Math Test 3**; reteach weak skills |
| **5** | RLA Reading — genres & strategies | Genre guide + reading lessons; begin hot-text practice |
| **6** | RLA Short Constructed Response | **SCR Mastery**; **RLA Test 1** + review |
| **7** | RLA Revising & Editing | Conventions refresher; **RLA Test 2**; **Math Test 4** |
| **8** | RLA Extended Constructed Response + final push | **ECR Mastery**; **RLA Tests 3 & 4**; final diagnostic review |

---

> **Watch Out:** Do not skip the review step. The biggest score gains come from understanding **why**
> you missed a question, then practicing that exact skill again. Your diagnostic charts point the way.
