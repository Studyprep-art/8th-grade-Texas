### About This Edition

This 2027 Edition reflects the STAAR program **as of November 5, 2026**, including the online test
format and the current question types, scoring, and TEKS blueprint. Under Texas House Bill 8 (2025),
**spring 2027 is the final STAAR administration** for grades 3–8; a new assessment, the Student
Success Tool, is scheduled to replace STAAR beginning in the 2027–2028 school year. This book is
built to prepare students thoroughly for that final STAAR year.

While every effort has been made to ensure accuracy, test specifications can change. Families and
educators should confirm current details on the official Texas Education Agency website.
